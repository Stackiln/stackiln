# Stackiln brand

The Stackiln mark combines stacked modules with the heat of a kiln: composable inputs become a durable, standalone product.

## Assets

- `brand/stackiln-mark.png` is the primary transparent mark for repository pages, avatars, and raster use.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Do not rotate, stretch, recolour individual pieces, add effects, or place the mark on a background that obscures its silhouette.

The working palette is ember orange `#FF9D1C`, kiln coral `#FF5A2B`, fired red `#EE3D2B`, and charcoal `#171717`. Product interfaces are not required to use this palette.
