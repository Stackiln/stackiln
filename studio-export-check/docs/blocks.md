# Home page blocks

Recipe: custom selection.

1. **Split image hero** (`hero.split-image`, split) — Editorial copy paired with a strong visual surface.
2. **Announcement bar** (`navigation.announcement-bar`, announcement) — A focused message for launches, updates, and time-sensitive news.
3. **Logo cloud** (`proof.logo-cloud`, grid) — A calm grid of customer and partner names.
4. **Icon feature grid** (`features.icon-grid`, icons) — Scannable capabilities in a responsive card grid.
5. **Editorial introduction** (`story.editorial-intro`, editorial) — A generous opening for mission and product context.
6. **Pricing cards** (`pricing.cards`, cards) — Three clear packages with consistent feature summaries.
7. **Testimonial cards** (`testimonials.cards`, cards) — A balanced grid of customer perspectives.
8. **FAQ accordion** (`faq.accordion`, accordion) — Expandable answers using native disclosure controls.
9. **Contact form** (`forms.contact`, contact) — A direct route for product and support enquiries.
10. **Mega footer** (`footer.mega`, mega) — A structured directory for larger product sites.
11. **Centred hero** (`hero.centered`, centered) — A direct proposition with a restrained action group.
