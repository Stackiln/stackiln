# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Stackiln a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that do not align with this Code of Conduct and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to `christianjamesrelf@gmail.com`. Do not open a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

Maintainers will use the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction for isolated unprofessional behaviour, a warning for a repeated or serious incident, a temporary ban for sustained inappropriate behaviour, and a permanent ban for a pattern of violations or harassment.
